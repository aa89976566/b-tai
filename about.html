About B Tai

B Tai is a place to discover Taiwan through the work of its independent artists. We select illustrations, original characters and everyday objects, and introduce the people and ideas behind them.

We are building B Tai for people abroad who want to get to know these creators, beginning with Europe. Alongside the collection, you will find introductions to each artist and explanations of the local references that may be unfamiliar outside Taiwan.

What we do

We select artist-designed objects, introduce the people behind them, and help independent retailers explore the collection.

Why Taiwan?

It is where our relationships with these creators begin. We introduce their individual perspectives, rather than asking every artist to represent one national style.

Our first selection features TungB studio. As B Tai develops, each new artist will have a dedicated introduction and a clearly identified collection.
